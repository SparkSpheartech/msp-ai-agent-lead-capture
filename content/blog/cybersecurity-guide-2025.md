Over 60% of small businesses that suffer a significant data breach close their doors permanently within six months. The era of believing "it won't happen to my business" is over.

## The DIY Trap

Many owners believe a simple antivirus subscription is enough. Modern threats like ransomware, business email compromise (BEC), and phishing attacks bypass basic desktop filters with ease.

## 3 Essentials Every Small Business Needs

1. **Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA):** The single most effective deterrent against unauthorized account access across your cloud software.
2. **Automated Immutable Backups:** Follow the 3-2-1 rule: 3 copies of data, 2 different media types, 1 offsite encrypted copy.
3. **Employee Security Awareness:** Regular security training remains your frontline defense against social engineering.
